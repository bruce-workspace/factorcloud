# FactorCloud Brand Messaging Guidelines
_March 2026 — Rule1 Ventures — CONFIDENTIAL_

## Positioning Statement
FactorCloud is the modern factoring platform that automates cash application, keeps books accurate to the penny with dual-ledger accounting, and migrates your entire book for free. We connect you to 20+ integrations your current platform will never support.

## The Three Unkillable Differentiators
Every piece of marketing must reinforce at least one of these.

1. **Automated Cash Application** — 90% automation rate. AI-powered matching. No manual remittance. No competitor does this.
2. **Dual-Ledger Accounting** — Every transaction recorded twice, balanced automatically, accurate to the penny. No other competitor leads with this.
3. **Free Migration and Onboarding** — We migrate clients, debtors, open AR, terms, fee structures. Run parallel. Train your team. No setup fees. No contracts.

## ICP
Mid-to-large factoring companies, 10+ employees, high invoice volume, transportation/staffing/construction/general. Already on a platform. Evaluating a switch because current software can't keep up.

**Decision makers:**
- Owner/Founder — trust and proof. IFA active. Allergic to BS.
- VP Ops / COO — lives in the software. Feels the pain.
- CFO / Controller — dual-ledger story, audit readiness, veto power.

## Brand Voice: The Competent Peer
Sound like the smartest operator in the room who built something impressive and is genuinely happy to show it.

**Seven Principles:**
1. Lead with what it does, not what it is. Not "modern cloud-based platform." Say "your cash applies itself."
2. Prove domain knowledge through casual specificity. Use: schedules, reserves, rebates, chargebacks, aging, NFE. Don't explain them.
3. Confident without aggressive. Show the work. Let them draw conclusions.
4. Sound like someone they'd sit next to at the IFA dinner. Not the guy on stage. The operator who built something.
5. Practical warmth, not corporate warmth. "We built free migration because switching is terrifying, and cost shouldn't be the reason you stay stuck."
6. Never use words the buyer wouldn't use. Not "leverage" or "empower." Say "this saves me two hours a day."
7. Short sentences hit harder. "Your auditors will thank you" > a paragraph about audit readiness.
